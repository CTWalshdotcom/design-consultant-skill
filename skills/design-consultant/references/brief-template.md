# Design brief template

Save as `design-brief-<asset>.md` beside the project's `_overview.md`. Run `date` before writing the date. Keep it to one screen where possible.

```markdown
# Design brief: <asset> for <project>

Date: <from date>
Mode: full design | quick review | build to standards
Design system: <path, or "none: new brand">
Status: draft | approved <date> | in rounds | done

## Goal
- For: <audience>
- They must: <understand / do / decide>
- They see it on: <phone, projector, store thumbnail, inbox, weekly review>
- Success is: <calls booked, installs, a decision made, a slide remembered>
- Neurodivergent audience: yes | no

## Must stay
- <elements, words or styling he protects>

## His taste for this asset
- <plain words (designer term)>
- Feels like: <words>  Never feels like: <words>
- Sliders: friendly-authoritative <n>, playful-serious <n>, simple-rich <n>, classic-fresh <n>, calm-energetic <n>

## The idea
On this <asset>, <something only it has> becomes <the visual device>.
Competitor test: <passed / rewritten because ...>

## Avoid
- <styles, devices or references to stay away from>

## Checks
- Success measure: <from the asset guide>
- Floor and inclusive checks: <full set | lighter set>

## Decisions log
- <date>: <what he chose, rejected or changed, and why>

## Open
- [ ] <facts or material still to get>
```
